## Helping tech, SaaS & Fintech companies build better products with AI-driven workflows. UX/UI | Product Design | Design System | AI Agents | Figma | Prototype | Usability | Interaction Design | UX Research | Discovery 👋

###### About

Designer with a background in visual communication, marketing, and technology, currently working in Product Design, with a focus on UX/UI.

My career started in graphic design and 360º advertising agencies, where I worked across different clients, brands, industries, deadlines, and business goals. Over time, I moved beyond visual execution to focus on understanding complex problems and turning business needs into digital solutions.

Experience & results:

• Outmarketing Brazil: led the implementation of interactive content projects using Ion by Rock Content, learning the platform and structuring the process in 1 month, then rolling it out across the company over the following 6 months. Delivered 20+ interactive assets — quizzes, assessments, calculators, and e-books — for 8+ technology clients.

• Degrau Publicidade: expanded my work into websites and digital products, incorporating AI into the design and production workflow.

• Boston Medical Group (BMG): led a pilot project starting from an open-ended business objective, structuring proto-personas, need statements, user journeys, and prioritization before moving into interface design.

• Design + AI: using Claude + Figma MCP + Design Systems, reduced the time required to create auction interfaces by 81%, from 16 hours to 3 hours.

• Content automation: integrated Claude, Figma, ClickUp, and Obsidian to automate content production for 8+ clients, enabling 3 months of deliverables to be produced in approximately 4 hours.

Today, I work at the intersection of UX/UI, Product Design, and Artificial Intelligence, with experience across technology, fintech, SaaS, edtech, and healthtech.

My approach is AI-First, Human-led: AI accelerates execution; design defines the problem, validates decisions, and shapes the experience.

I focus on digital products, complex systems, and experiences that combine design expertise, business thinking, Design Systems, and strategic AI adoption to build solutions, optimize processes, and generate measurable impact.
